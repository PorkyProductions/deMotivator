(de)Motivator has a few hidden surprises tucked away for curious users. Here's what we're willing to reveal (and what we'll leave for you to discover).

---

## The Hedgehog

The hedgehog is PorkyProductions' mascot (based off the real life Porky the Hedgehog owned by Tristan!) — you'll spot it in the footer, on login pages, and elsewhere around the app. But it has a bigger role than just decoration.

There's a **hidden hedgehog page** somewhere in the app. Finding it unlocks the **Hog Hunter** 🦔 achievement and rewards you with a chaotic, animated surprise. We won't tell you exactly how to get there, but pay close attention to interactive elements on pages you might not normally click around on.

Hint: some things become interesting if you're persistent enough.

---

## The Spinning Hedgehog

The hedgehog icon in the footer isn't just sitting there. Try interacting with it — press and hold (or click and hold) to watch it react. The longer you hold, the more dramatic the effect gets.

---

## The 404 Page

Most apps have a boring 404 error page. (de)Motivator's is... different.

If you navigate to a page that doesn't exist, you'll land on a custom 404 page with a **Story Mode**. Hit the start button and watch an automated, dramatically-paced narrative unfold — complete with confetti, emoticons, and a surprisingly theatrical telling of what went wrong. You can pause, skip forward, or restart the story at any point.

It's worth visiting at least once. Try going to [demotivator.web.app/this-does-not-exist](https://demotivator.web.app/this-does-not-exist) to see it in action.

---

## Dark Mode

The app supports dark mode automatically based on your system preferences. There's no manual toggle — it follows whatever your device or browser is set to.
