## PorkyProductionsID

A **PorkyProductionsID** is a free account that works across all PorkyProductions products. Creating one unlocks everything the app has to offer:

- Syncing your progress and settings between devices.
- Insult streak tracking.
- Achievements for hitting insult milestones and more.
- Access to the leaderboard.
- The ability to browse all insults and save favorites.
- The ability to submit insult requests.
- Personalized settings that follow you everywhere.

You can sign up with an email and password or with Google. Head to the **Sign Up** page to create your account, or the **Login** page if you already have one.

## Your Account Page

Once signed in, the **Account** page is your profile hub. Here you'll find:

- **Your avatar** — Generated automatically from your account info.
- **Insults Seen** — A running total of every insult you've generated on the home page.
- **Insult Streak** — How many consecutive days you've generated at least one insult.
- **Achievements** — A grid showing which achievements you've earned and which are still locked.

Use the **Refresh Profile Data** button to pull the latest stats from the server. The Account Hub sidebar also gives you quick links to Settings, the Leaderboard, and the Insult Browser.

You can **sign out** or **delete your account** from this page as well.

## Insult Streaks

Every day you generate at least one insult (outside of MEGAMODE), your **insult streak** goes up by one. Miss a day and your streak resets to 1 the next time you use the app.

Streaks are tracked automatically in the background — just keep pressing that button daily and your streak will climb. Your current streak is visible on your Account page.

## Achievements

Achievements are milestones you unlock by using the app. Once earned, they're permanently saved to your account and displayed on your profile. Here's every achievement available:

| Achievement | Emoji | How to Unlock |
| ----------- | ----- | ------------- |
| **First Blood** | 🔥 | Generate your first insult. |
| **Roast Regular** | 💯 | Generate 1,000 insults. |
| **Low Life** | 👹 | Generate 10,000 insults. |
| **Disparagement Demi-God** | 🌋 | Generate 100,000 insults. |
| **Ego Death** | 💀 | Generate 1,000,000 insults. |
| **Week of Spite** | 📆 | Keep a 7-day insult streak. |
| **Monthly Menace** | 🗓️ | Keep a 30-day insult streak. |
| **Centurion of Cynicism** | 💥 | Keep a 100-day insult streak. |
| **A Year of Suffering** | 🕰️ | Keep a 365-day insult streak. |
| **Hog Hunter** | 🦔 | Find the hidden hedgehog easter egg. (See [Secrets](/docs/web-app/web-secrets).) |
| **Share the Hate** | 📢 | Share an insult on social media. |
| **Top Three Terror** | 🏆 | Reach the top 3 on the leaderboard. |
| **Supreme Leader of (de)Motivation** | 👑 | Claim 1st place on the leaderboard. |

Insult milestone achievements (First Blood through Ego Death) are **retroactive** — if you already have 5,000 insults and then sign in, you'll automatically receive First Blood and Roast Regular.

Streak milestone achievements (Week of Spite through A Year of Suffering) are also **retroactive** — refreshing your profile syncs your current streak and awards every streak milestone you've already reached.

Earned achievements appear in full color on your profile; locked ones are shown as disabled so you can see what to aim for.

## Leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** ranks every signed-in user by total insults generated. The top three positions earn medal icons (🥇🥈🥉) and each entry shows the user's avatar and insult count.

Your own entry is highlighted so you can quickly spot your rank. The leaderboard can be refreshed manually with the button at the top of the page, and the timestamp shows when it was last updated.

Reaching the top 3 or claiming 1st place unlocks the corresponding achievements (**Top Three Terror** and **Supreme Leader of (de)Motivation**).
