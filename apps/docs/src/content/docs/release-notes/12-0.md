## (de)Motivator v12 Change Log

*Release Date: June 21, 2023*

It's finally here bozos! After countless hours of creative torment, PorkyProductions proudly presents (de)Motivator v12, a monumental update that will leave you utterly flabbergasted. As the summer sun shines upon us, we invite you to dive into the depths of (de)Motivator and unleash your inner demons. Happy summer, fellow sufferers!

### New Features:

- **New Insults**: Throw yourself even deeper into your pit of dispair, with a kinda large amount of new insults!
- **Insult tracking**: Keep tabs on all the insults you dish out and receive. The more, the merrier!
- **Leaderboard**: Compete with your fellow insult aficionados and claim the title of the most lifeless, soulless, saddest, and least motivated person in the universe
- **Copy insult to clipboard functionality**: Now you can easily share your favorite insults with friends, enemies, and everyone in between.
- **Profane insults option in view all**: Unleash your inner rebel with a collection of profanely creative insults. Viewer discretion advised!
- **Improved dark mode**: Dive deeper into the abyss of despair with an even more visually captivating dark mode experience.
- **Validation system for email/password login to improve security**: Protect your precious insults from prying eyes with a fortified login system.

### Changes:

- **Color and design system got an overhaul**: Prepare to be visually assaulted by a fresh, eye-catching color palette and design elements that are both revolting and mesmerizing.
- **Improved and expanded use of icons**: We've enlisted an army of hilariously sarcastic icons to enrich your insult-inducing experience.
- **Improved login functionality**: Logging in is now smoother than butter on a hot skillet. No more excuses for not spreading your verbal torment.
- **DX improvements**: We've sprinkled some magical dust on the developer experience, making it even more delightful to create and maintain (de)Motivator-compatible applications.
- **Move from Apache to ISC license**: We've swapped our license like a magician swaps hats. The (de)Motivator is now licensed under the ISC license, setting the stage for a world of merciless insults.

### Removed:

- **Desktop port of the app** (see discussions tab): Bid farewell to the desktop version as it goes down in flames. Join the discussions to mourn its untimely demise.

So go forth, you wretched souls, and embrace the power of insults with (de)Motivator v12. May your words cut like a thousand daggers and your laughter echo through the realms of misery! Happy summer, and may your sunny days be filled with sizzling burns!

~ The (de)Motivator team @PorkyProductions
